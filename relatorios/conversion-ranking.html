Where is Portugal's 2030 biomethane? The existing biogas fleet, plant by plant

34 biogas power plants of at least 1 MW on the DGEG list · owner reports read for 29 · ranked by verdict, then value

Answer. The Action Plan calls converting the existing biogas plants a "no regret" first step, to follow the end of their electricity tariffs (2026–2027). On installed engines and fleet-average hours, the 34 listed plants would inject about 1,029 GWh/yr of biomethane; on owner-published output for the 29 plants that have it (and the calibration for the other 5), 571 GWh/yr: the plants with owner data make 51% of what the calibration gave them. 8 of 34 convert profitably at the €62/MWh reference or once their tariff ends: about 257 GWh/yr, 9% of the Plan's 2.72 TWh for 2030 (its implementation potential, 9% of grid gas), all of it on owner-published output. 7 of those plants (234 GWh/yr) are already being converted by their owners; 1 (24 GWh/yr: Aterro Sanitario de Palmela) has no public plan. EGF (Mota-Engil) owns 23 of the 34 plants and 77% of the convertible volume.

4. Licensing path for a conversion

A conversion is one project typology, and its path is shorter than a new plant's: the site, its environmental licence and its land use are already settled. 14 steps across four phases; how many apply depends on the owner and the plant:

  • Aterro do Gestal - Canedo (EGF (Mota-Engil), landfill): 13 steps, 11 entities — Owner, ERSAR, Concession grantor, DGEG, APA, APA / CCDR, Município, Network operator, ANEPC, National system or a recognised voluntary scheme, Issuing body for gas guarantees of origin.
  • Central de Biogás da Abrunheira (CDA) (Municipal / inter-municipal, digester): 11 steps, 9 entities — Owner, DGEG, APA, APA / CCDR, Município, Network operator, ANEPC, National system or a recognised voluntary scheme, Issuing body for gas guarantees of origin.
  • ETAR de Frielas (Autoconsumo) (Water utility, digester, power used on site): 13 steps, 11 entities — Owner, ERSAR, Concession grantor, DGEG, APA, APA / CCDR, Município, Network operator, ANEPC, National system or a recognised voluntary scheme, Issuing body for gas guarantees of origin.

5. Method and limits

  • Every figure comes from the conversion engine (`packages/engine/src/conversion.ts`), the same model as the one-page briefs and the deep packs: biomethane from engine output ÷ efficiency, membrane upgrading cost curve, connection to the nearest mapped transmission line × 1.3, electricity margin given up.
  • Landfill gas declines 3% a year; nitrogen and oxygen removal is not costed, so landfill cases are optimistic.
  • Each plant's own tariff and end date are not public except where the owner states them; the study reports the feed-in contracts ending in 2026–2027.
  • Volumes are first-year biomethane injected (HHV). The Action Plan's 2030 figure is a potential across all feedstocks, not a conversion target.
  • No owner, operator or authority was contacted. Stage and plans come from public reports and the LNEG register.
